How It Works

Three Steps to Interview Ready

Upload your details once, practice with a AI interviewer, and get a full improvement report — in under 30 minutes.

01
Upload Resume + Job Description

Paste the actual JD you are applying for and upload your resume. The AI reads both and builds an interview specifically for that role — not a generic one.

02
Practice with Your AI Interviewer

Speak your answers out loud to a AI interviewer. It adapts questions based on your responses — just like a real interview panel would.

03
Get Your Full Report

Instantly receive a breakdown — what you answered well, grammar issues, filler words, confidence score, pacing, and exactly what to improve before the real day.
